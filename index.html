HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No queue.

1. Scan or search

Point your camera at a product's QR code, or search the live catalogue if it's easier — stock and price update in real time.

2. Build your basket

Adjust quantities, remove items, and watch your running total the whole way through the store — no surprises at the end.

3. Pay & walk out

Pay with EcoCash, bank card, or cash at a till — get a digital receipt instantly, no queue required.
